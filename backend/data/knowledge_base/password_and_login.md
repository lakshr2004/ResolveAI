# Password Reset and Login Support
Customers can reset a password from the Sign In page using the Forgot Password link. Enter the email address registered to the account and follow the reset link delivered by email. Reset links expire after 30 minutes and can only be used once. If no email arrives, check spam and confirm the email address is correct. After five consecutive failed login attempts, the account is temporarily locked for 15 minutes. Wait for the lockout period to end before trying again. Support agents must verify account ownership before changing registered email addresses.
